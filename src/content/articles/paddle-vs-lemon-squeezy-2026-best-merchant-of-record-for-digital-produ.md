Paddle and Lemon Squeezy charge exactly the same fee.

Both publish the same headline rate: 5% + 50¢ per transaction, with no monthly fee. So the fee alone will not decide this for you. What decides it is how each one treats cheap products, what comes bundled, and the fact that Lemon Squeezy is in the middle of moving its sellers to Stripe.

This comparison assumes you sell a digital product, a SaaS subscription, or an online course and want someone else to handle global sales tax. That is what a merchant of record does: it becomes the legal seller, collects and pays VAT and sales tax, and takes on that liability. Both platforms do this.

## What does each platform charge per sale?

The same: 5% + 50¢. Paddle lists it as the rate for its Pay-as-you-go plan, per checkout transaction, with "no monthly fees, migration fees, or hidden extras." Lemon Squeezy lists 5% + 50¢ on its Standard plan and notes that some payments may carry additional fees.

Both offer custom pricing above that. Paddle has a Custom pricing tier for high-volume businesses. Lemon Squeezy offers volume pricing through sales.

## What happens if your product costs under $10?

The fixed 50¢ hurts. On a $5 product, 5% + 50¢ is 75¢, a 15% effective fee. On a $10 product it is $1, or 10%. On a $50 product it drops to $3, or 6%.

Paddle states that if you sell products under $10, you can contact it for bespoke pricing. Lemon Squeezy publishes no such option for low-price items. If you sell $5 templates or icon packs, that is a real reason to ask Paddle for a quote.

## What is happening with Lemon Squeezy and Stripe?

Stripe bought Lemon Squeezy in 2024. On January 28, 2026, Lemon Squeezy's CEO wrote that the team has been building Stripe Managed Payments, Stripe's own merchant of record service, and that the goal is to give Lemon Squeezy users an easy way to migrate to it. Stripe opened Managed Payments to all digital businesses at its Sessions conference in late April 2026. Lemon Squeezy has not announced a shutdown date, and its sign-up page still accepts new sellers.

The same post admits to slower support and fewer product updates while the team worked on the new product. For a new seller, that means you would likely move platforms at some point. Plan for it before you build your checkout around Lemon Squeezy.

## What else comes with each one?

Paddle bundles checkout, subscriptions, tax and regulatory compliance, fraud protection, chargebacks and buyer support, reporting, and a migration service. It also lists Retain, its tool for reducing churn. Paddle says there are no lock-in periods and that your data can be moved to another provider.

Lemon Squeezy includes tax and EU VAT handling, fraud protection, and email marketing free up to 500 subscribers. Payouts go out twice a month by bank wire or PayPal, to more than 200 countries. For a solo creator, the built-in email list is one less tool to pay for.

## The verdict: which platform should you choose?

**Pick Paddle if** you are starting now. The rate is the same, there is no platform move ahead of you, and products under $10 can get custom pricing. Subscription businesses can also use Paddle Retain to cut churn.

**Pick Lemon Squeezy if** you already sell there and it works, or you want the free email marketing for a small list. Expect to move to Stripe Managed Payments later, and keep your product data easy to export.

We would pick Paddle for a new digital product today. Same price, fewer unknowns.
