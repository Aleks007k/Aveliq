Most buyers optimize for the wrong constraint.

If you need to turn a prompt into a polished deck in under two minutes and you don't care about per-seat billing, Gamma is almost certainly the answer. If your team needs brand governance, real-time collaboration, and a flat per-user price they can expense without surprises, Beautiful.ai is the cleaner choice. The decision comes down to whether your bottleneck is speed of creation or consistency of output across a team.

## Is There a Free Tier?

Gamma has one; Beautiful.ai does not. Gamma's permanent free plan gives you 400 one-time AI credits (they don't refresh) and lets you generate up to 10 slides per prompt with no credit card required. Beautiful.ai offers a 14-day free trial with unlimited feature access, but it requires a credit card and ends.

That asymmetry matters for how you evaluate each product. With Gamma, you can test the core AI generation loop at zero cost and zero commitment. With Beautiful.ai, the clock starts the moment you sign up, and the trial is designed to convert you before you've had time to stress-test it against a real project.

## What Does Gamma Actually Cost?

Gamma publishes its plan structure clearly, even if the exact USD figures are geo-localized and may vary by region. Plus and Pro are available on monthly or annual billing. Ultra is monthly only with no annual option. Business is per-seat, annual billing only. One line in the pricing tells the story: if you want the most advanced AI models — text, image, and video — and 20× more AI usage, that sits behind the Ultra tier, which you cannot lock in at an annual rate.

Gamma also offers a Team plan (annual, 2-seat minimum) below Business (annual, 10-seat minimum).

On paid plans, standard deck generation does not consume credits. Credits are spent on Agent edits, premium model usage, and API calls. That changes the calculus: most everyday use won't drain your allowance the way the free plan's 400 one-time credits might suggest.

The free plan's 400 credits are one-time, not recurring. Power users will hit that ceiling before they've properly evaluated the product. Worth knowing before you invest time building a workflow around it.

## What Does Beautiful.ai Actually Cost?

Beautiful.ai publishes its prices clearly. The individual Pro plan runs $14.50 per month billed annually — there is no monthly billing option for Pro. A single presentation is available for a one-time fee of $45 if you don't need a subscription. The Team plan is $40 per user per month billed annually, or $50 per user billed monthly, with seats capped at 2 to 20. Students with a valid .edu email get a year free.

There is no published price for a plan beyond 20 seats, which means larger organizations would need to contact sales. For individuals, $14.50/month (annual) is a straightforward number to model. For teams, $40 per user per month (billed annually) is the figure to put in a budget.

## The Case for Gamma

Gamma's strongest argument is breadth of output type. Beyond presentations, it generates docs, websites, social posts, and images from a single prompt. That matters if you're a solo operator or a small team producing content across formats, not just slide decks. If you also rely on AI writing tools, it's worth reading [how Jasper and Copy.ai compare on the creative content side](/articles/jasper-vs-copy-ai-2026-which-ai-creative-tool-is-worth-your-money/) before committing to any single platform.

The credit rollover system on paid plans (unused credits roll over up to 2× your monthly allowance) rewards consistent use without punishing a slow month. API access on Pro, Ultra, and Business opens up workflow integrations that Beautiful.ai doesn't advertise. Export options — PDF, PPTX, PNG, and Google Slides — are available on all plans, including free.

Because standard deck generation doesn't touch credits on paid tiers, the anxiety around running out is largely confined to the free plan. Once you're paying, credits are a cost for premium features, not a meter on your core workflow.

## The Case for Beautiful.ai

Beautiful.ai's pitch is design consistency at scale. Over 300 Smart Slide layouts constrain how slides can look, which sounds limiting until you've seen what a 20-person team produces without guardrails. Unlimited AI content generation on paid plans means no credit accounting at all.

For teams specifically, the combination of custom branded themes, real-time collaborative editing, and engagement analytics covers the three things a deck-heavy organization actually needs: brand governance, parallel work, and feedback on whether anyone read the thing. The 2-to-20 seat constraint on Team is worth flagging — if you're above that, the path forward isn't clear from published pricing.

The requirement to enter a credit card for even a 14-day trial is a minor but real signal about the conversion strategy. It's not unusual, but it's worth knowing before you click "start free trial." ## Which Plan Is Cheapest for a Solo User?

Gamma's free plan is the cheapest option available: $0, no card, 400 credits. If you exhaust those and need a paid plan, the USD price depends on your region and what Gamma's geo-localized page shows you. Beautiful.ai's individual Pro plan at $14.50/month (billed annually) is the lowest published paid price in this comparison. For a solo user who wants unlimited AI generation and a predictable annual cost, that $14.50/month rate is the clearest number on the table. If a full subscription feels like overkill, the $45 one-time single presentation option is an alternative worth considering.

## Verdict

Solo users should start on Gamma's free plan to test the AI generation loop at no cost, then compare the paid tier price on their local version of gamma.app against Beautiful.ai's $14.50/month (annual) before committing. If format breadth and API access matter, Gamma's paid plans are the stronger fit. If unlimited AI generation and a predictable annual bill are what you need, Beautiful.ai Pro is the cleaner answer.

For teams of 2 to 20, the comparison narrows to $40/seat/month (Beautiful.ai, annual) against Gamma's Business plan pricing as shown on your regional page. Beautiful.ai's Team plan adds real-time collaboration, custom branded themes, and engagement analytics — features that justify the per-seat cost if brand consistency is a genuine requirement. Gamma's Business tier is the better fit if your team produces content across multiple formats and needs API integrations. For students, Beautiful.ai's free year with a .edu email is the most generous offer in this comparison by a wide margin. Use it.
